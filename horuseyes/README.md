## 👁️ What is HorusEye?

**HorusEye** is an open-source, immersive cyber-exploration command sanctuary designed with an **Ancient Egyptian Cyber-Gold aesthetic (Neo-Kemet Cyberpunk)**. Deep obsidian blacks and radiant royal golds replace ordinary interfaces to deliver a sacred, futuristic observation experience.

It unifies the **50 most legendary, rabbit holes of the global internet** into **Halos of Vision**: circular starlight portals with ambient hieroglyphic runes that reveal live, atmospheric dream-like previews when hovered over, and open instantaneously upon clicking.

It also connects directly to **Osiris AI** live planetary intelligence through an animated **Ancient Papyrus Scroll Unroll Transition**.

## ⚡ What's New in v4.5 Kemet

1. **𓂀 Interactive Eye of Horus Cursor Tracker**:
   - The sacred Wedjat eye in the top bar dynamically tracks your cursor or touch gaze across the entire screen using real-time linear interpolation (*lerp*).
   - Clicking on the eye triggers a dilation with an auditory focus pulse.

2. **🪐 Halos of Vision (Clean, No Clutter)**:
   - Cluttered text buttons ("preview", "enter") have been eliminated.
   - Hovering over any halo triggers a **brutal Egyptian visual effect**: celestial outer rings spin, a golden solar sunburst aura rises, and the central preview visual softens into an ethereal, dreamy mirage.
   - **Click anywhere on the halo** to immediately awaken the portal!
   - Bold, heavy gold typography (*Syne* & *Cinzel Decorative*) ensures maximum readability and regal contrast.

3. **𓁀 The Sanctuary of Osiris (Papyrus Scroll Transition)**:
   - Dedicated header access to [Osiris AI Live Intelligence](https://osirisai.live/?layers=maritime,satellites,cctv,cctv_previews,live_news,earthquakes,global_incidents,day_night,cables,sdk_sea,sdk_air,sdk_naval).
   - Clicking **`𓁀 OSIRIS AI`** unrolls an animated ancient Egyptian papyrus scroll across the screen with authentic hieroglyphic sound design before transitioning into the live telemetry realm.

4. **𓏛 Real-Time Bilingual Toggle (English Default / Spanish)**:
   - English is now the default language across the entire sanctuary.
   - A single click on the hieroglyphic **`𓏛 EN | ES`** button instantly translates all UI components, category streams, tips, and descriptions in real time.

5. **𓏢 Subtle Egyptian Ambient Music (Procedural BGM)**:
   - Built directly with the Web Audio API without heavy or broken MP3 files.
   - Generates a gentle, low-volume Ancient Egyptian Phrygian drone and soft Ney flute harmonics.
   - Low ambient volume so it enhances the experience without overpowering your music or workflow. Toggle anytime with **`𓏢 BGM`**.

6. **🎨 Pure Royal Gold Palette (No Sky-Blue)**:
   - Eliminated distracting cyan and sky-blue colors.
   - Styled in pure pharaonic gold (`#ffd700`, `#ffea79`, `#dfa200`), dark bronze, and obsidian blacks.

7. **🛡️ Curated 50 Portals & Anti-CSP Protection**:
   - Filtered out mundane administrative websites and integrated top-tier rabbit holes like **Radio Garden**, **Ancient Earth Globe**, **Drive & Listen**, and **NightEarth**.
   - Intelligent fallbacks provide instant 1-click teleportation for websites that enforce `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`.

8. **Favicon Icon**:
   - Embedded golden Eye of Horus SVG favicon for browser tabs.
